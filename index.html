What is FlowChat?

FlowChat is a live chat experience designed to connect website visitors with your support or sales team.

Does Get Started open the login page?

Yes. Every main Get Started call-to-action on this page links directly to login.php.

Can I use the live preview without logging in?

Yes. The Try Live Preview button opens the demonstration chat directly on the page.
